Snakeoil

Snakeoil is a cocktail bar wearing a dive bar’s jacket. Tucked behind Kings Heath High Street, it’s made for good music, late nights and another round.

The drinks are measured to the millilitre. Everything else is a bit loose. No dress code, no ten-page menu, no lecture about the ice. House cocktails, a cold Modelo on tap and a back bar that knows what it’s doing.

Walk in. Sit down. We’ll sort you out.

Come one. Come all.

The Drinks

House cocktails, familiar favourites with a twist, and Modelo on tap. Pick your poison.
